# Landing background video — source footage & license

The landing loop (`bg-land.mp4` / `bg-port.mp4`, 15 s seamless) is edited from three
aerial clips by **Logan Voss** (https://www.pexels.com/@logan/), all published under the
**Pexels License** (free for commercial use, no attribution required, modification allowed):

1. https://www.pexels.com/video/stunning-aerial-view-of-kauai-s-coastal-beauty-28615879/
2. https://www.pexels.com/video/stunning-aerial-view-of-tropical-coastline-28616126/
3. https://www.pexels.com/video/stunning-aerial-views-of-kauai-s-shipwreck-beach-28615727/

Edit (2026-07-18): 3 s windows from each source (QHD 2560x1440@60fps), half-speed to 30 fps,
per-clip color grade to a matched turquoise daylight look (clip 3's backlit glare compressed
and cooled), 1 s cross-dissolves, frame-exact loop trim (end frame is the source frame
immediately before the start frame). Posters are the first frame of each orientation.
