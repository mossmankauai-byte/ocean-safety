Privacy in the GoHawaii version

Effective 23 September 2026 · Version 1.0

This page covers the version of the app you reach from GoHawaii (links with ?ref=gohawaii), run by Tropical Agronomics LLC dba OceanSafe for GoHawaii visitors. There are no accounts, no signup and no login. Nobody types a name into this app.

The short version

  • No analytics service, no crash reports, no ads, no cookies for tracking.
  • The app sends daily visit totals to a GoHawaii counter. Totals only: no id, no location, nothing that tells one visitor from another. Off if your browser sends Do Not Track.
  • If you tap Locate Me, a position rounded to about 1 km goes to our server for your local forecast and is not kept.
  • Photos and map tiles load from the sites that host them, and those sites see your IP address.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps a few things in your browser's own storage so it works the next time you open it: the island you picked, advisories you have already dismissed, the latest GoHawaii posts and place details, and which pop-ups you have seen this visit. None of it is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it.

The visit totals

While you use this version, the app adds up a few things in the page and sends the totals when you leave, or every 30 seconds:

  • that a visit happened, and on which island (Kauaʻi, Oʻahu, Maui or Hawaiʻi Island)
  • which beaches and which GoHawaii places were opened
  • which tabs were used
  • taps out to other sites, as one of three kinds: gohawaii.com, a listing's own website, or directions
  • how many advisory pop-ups were shown
  • the hour of the visit, the language your browser reads in (two letters, such as EN or JA) and the kind of device (iPhone or iPad, Android, or computer)

The server adds these to counts per day in Hawaiʻi time and keeps nothing else. There is no id, no cookie for this, no location and no list of visits, so nobody, including us and GoHawaii staff, can pick out one visitor. GoHawaii and State and county staff see the totals in their Dashboard.

Do Not Track

If your browser sends Do Not Track, the app sends no visit totals at all. It still loads GoHawaii posts and places, because that is the content of the app.

Children

The app collects nothing that identifies anyone, of any age.

Contact

Questions: privacy@oceansafety.app. The policy for the rest of the app, outside the GoHawaii version, is at ocean-safety.netlify.app/privacy.
