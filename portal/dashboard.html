When an order comes in
  • The payment goes straight into your own Square account, so Square's usual payment notifications and receipts apply — you'll see it in your Square Dashboard or Square app. It arrives as a payment, not an order ticket, so it won't show up in a POS order queue.
  • The order shows up under Recent orders above — pickup orders with the pickup code the customer shows you; ship orders say Ship, and the ship-to address is on the payment in your Square Dashboard. Your Sales page keeps the full history.
  • Once order email is switched on, we'll also email you each order — pickup orders include the pickup code; ship orders point you to the address on the order in your Square Dashboard.
  • Rental orders show their return time here; the pickup code doubles as the claim ticket. Deposits and any reminder texts run in your Square, not through OceanSafe.
