The honest math

  • Every figure is from our model of a 250-room property at 75% occupancy, with a 40–60% haircut for spending that would have happened anyway. Nothing here is measured at your property yet — the pilot does that.
  • Base case: roughly two times the license back in contribution — modeled against a mid-band (~$1,000/mo) license; the larger bands narrow the multiple.
  • Low case (15% of guests open the app): the model only washes against the license. We say that plainly rather than claim profit-center status.
  • In our model, tour-desk earnings plus one bad-weather week of Stay-Close alone plausibly cover the license.
