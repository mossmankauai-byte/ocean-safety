The ROI math

It doesn’t need to drive sales to pay for itself.

It only needs to drive a handful of incremental tours. Plug in your close rate and your contract value — the math holds at conservative industry numbers.

Per room
1 tour / 3 yrs

One incremental attended tour per room every three years pays the license — for that room, for life. At a ~15% close on ~$18k net, that single tour is worth ~$2,700 against the license cost over those 3 years. ~9× covered.

Per property · 200 units
~1.5 sales/yr

License is billed per room. Break-even on cash closed is one sale every eight months — or ~7 attended tours on expected value. Across 9,000–12,000 annual stays, that’s ~1 stay in 1,500 routing into a tour that wouldn’t have happened. A rounding error.

Per portfolio · ~886 units
<1 tour / wk

A 4-property MVW Kauaʻi footprint on the paid plan. Across 40,000+ annual stays, break-even is ~30–50 incremental tours for the whole portfolio for the year — under one extra tour per property per week.

Straight answers

?We already have our own app.+

?Privacy — guests didn’t sign up to be marketed to.+
The guest self-selects by scanning a QR and choosing their own path — opt-in by design, not a scrape. We collect only what they volunteer to book a session. No fulfillment means no payment data on our side. You own the guest relationship under your existing privacy terms.

Agreed completely — which is why the guardrail is hard-coded, not a policy. The owner QR routes to owner update only: points, add-a-week, upgrades, referral. There is no “buy ownership” path in the owner experience to accidentally trigger. Verify it yourself in the demo in 30 seconds.
