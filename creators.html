Program rules

The fine print, kept short.

  • You earn 50% of the commission GetYourGuide pays on tours booked through the app by visitors who arrived on your link — an even split — rising to 60% after 25 bookings in a quarter. Booking data comes from GetYourGuide's partner reporting and posts to your dashboard monthly.
  • Payouts monthly, $25 minimum (smaller balances roll over). A completed W-9 is required before your first payout; we issue a 1099-NEC if your year passes $600.
  • Disclose commissioned or incentivized posts with #ad. Don't make safety claims in your own voice — point to the app's live data instead.
  • OceanSafe covers Kauaʻi, Oʻahu, and Maui. Please don't present it as covering other islands.
  • Either side can end participation anytime; earned commission through the end date still pays out. We may decline or remove accounts that misrepresent the app or the program.
