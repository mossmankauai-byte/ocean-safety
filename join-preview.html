Common questions

The details, up front.

How fast can I be live?

Most partners go live within the same week. You claim your spot, send us your logo, and we build your co-branded page while your printed QR cards ship to your door.

What does the 90 / 10 split actually mean?

If you sell through the in-app store, you keep 90% of every sale and OceanSafe keeps 10%. That 10% is the only deduction — in year one we also cover card-processing fees, so nothing else comes out.

I don't sell products. Is this still worth it?

Yes. For hotels, inns, and rentals the value is the safety touch itself — your name on the app every guest checks, branded cards in rooms and at the desk, and fewer worried calls. The store is optional.

Is the safety information reliable?

Conditions come from public NOAA and NWS sources and refresh daily. OceanSafe is guidance, not a guarantee — it doesn't replace lifeguards or posted advisories, and your page says so clearly.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Cancel anytime. We email you before any renewal, so there are no surprise charges.
