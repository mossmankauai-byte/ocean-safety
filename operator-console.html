How we protect your guests

Restraint is the feature.

  • No names, room numbers, IP, or User-Agent stored
  • No persistent user ID, no ad pixels, no session replay
  • Opaque 8-byte session IDs · Do-Not-Track respected
  • Your property’s data only — no leaderboards, no cross-property attribution
Counts are a floor by design: privacy-respecting (DNT) guests aren’t tracked, and first-opens undercount repeat in-stay use. We’d rather show you a number you can trust than a bigger one you can’t.
Reading this page: every tile is tagged Measured / Modeled / Assumption / Sample. In this preview, all figures are Sample — illustrative placeholders, not your guests. Live mode renders only measured telemetry; a period with no data shows an empty state, never a seed number and never a movement figure against an empty prior week.

What makes it real: (1) build the endpoint-injection step so co-branded variants post to the live Worker (today they ship disabled), (2) deploy the Worker + seed per-slug keys, (3) ship a co-branded variant with the live endpoint, (4) accumulate 60+ days of rollups. Until then this is a layout preview by design.

Places on your guest app

Curate the beaches, trails, food & shops your QR-branded app recommends.

Set each place to ★ Featured (your house picks, surfaced first), ✓ Listed (in the app as normal), or hide it from your branded build. Your choices export as a config file applied to your co-branded app only — they never change anyone else's. Nothing here is live guest data.

Safety guardrail. Hazardous spots — beaches with no lifeguard, warning-only beaches, and rip/surge channels — can be moved off your recommended lists (⊘ Not recommended), but can't be deleted from a safety app. Their hazard warning always stays visible if a guest searches for them. Calm, guarded beaches and all food/shops/trails can be hidden fully.
Changes save to this browser as you go. Export config produces the places.json file that gets applied to your co-branded build — until then, edits are a local draft. Hazard locks are derived from the live OceanSafety beach dataset and can't be overridden here.
