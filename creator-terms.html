Creator Terms

Version: creator-terms-2026-07-18 · These are the terms you accept when you claim a creator link.

The deal

You share your OceanSafe link; when visitors who arrive on your link book a tour inside the app, you earn 50% of the commission GetYourGuide pays OceanSafe on that booking — an even split — rising to 60% after 25 credited bookings in a calendar quarter. Bookings are reconciled monthly from GetYourGuide partner reporting and posted to your dashboard.

Payouts

Paid monthly once your balance passes $25; smaller balances roll over. A completed W-9 is required before your first payout, and we issue a 1099-NEC if your payouts pass $600 in a year. Taxes on your earnings are your responsibility.

You're independent

Joining the program doesn't make you an employee, agent, or representative of OceanSafe. You can't make commitments on OceanSafe's behalf, and either of us can end participation at any time — commission you've already earned still pays out.

The safety rule

This one matters most. Never state that a beach, day, or activity is "safe" in your own voice. Point people to the app and let its live data do the talking. Ocean conditions are information, not a guarantee, and the app's warnings must never be cropped out, contradicted, or paraphrased into a promise.

Disclosure

Posts that promote your link are commissioned content. You are responsible for FTC disclosure — mark them #ad (or your platform's paid-partnership label).

Your name and photo

While you're in the program, you give OceanSafe permission to display the handle, display name, picks, and any profile photo you add — in your co-branded guide, on your dashboard, and nowhere else. Leave the program and this permission ends with it.

Changes and enforcement

We may adjust program rates and rules prospectively — with notice, never retroactively. We may disable accounts that misrepresent the app, the program, or ocean safety; earned commission still pays out.
