When a beach turns dangerous

CountingSample

Share of visitors who changed their plan after the app showed them a verdict, and where they went instead.

How this is computed
A plan change is a visitor who opened a beach with a hazard verdict and then opened a calmer beach on the same island within the same visit. Named flows are the County sample set for Kauaʻi; the other islands fill in once counting is on.

CountingSampleCounty 3 · State 3

How many visitors considered a beach while the app had it flagged red or yellow. Counted the day it happens.

How this is computed
Beach cards opened while the verdict shown at that moment was red or yellow, per beach, over the window. Counted the same way as a check; the verdict is the one the visitor saw.

Sunscreen scans (Act 104)

CountingSampleCounty 18 · 19 · State 20 · 21

What share of scanned sunscreen carries a filter banned from sale in Hawaiʻi, and which retail areas are worth a spot check. Areas only, never a store.

How this is computed
Every scan gets a verdict from the app's Act 104 filter list. Retail areas are the app's own district for where the scan happened; any area under ten scans is suppressed, and no store is ever named.
