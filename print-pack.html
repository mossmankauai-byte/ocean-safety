For the print shop

  • Files are sized to trim (no bleed) at 300 dpi. For borderless printing, ask the shop to scale to bleed — all critical content sits well inside every edge by design.
  • Cards: 14–16pt cardstock, matte or soft-touch. Poster: 100lb gloss/satin. Sticker: die-cut vinyl circle with matte laminate.
