Chapter one

The Guest App: What Your Guests Actually Use

Your guests get a straight answer to “is the beach safe today?” before they ask your front desk.

This is the live app, running right now. A guest opens it on any phone, no download, no account, and sees a map of Kauai with a safety flag on every beach: green, orange, or red. Surf, wind, and rain sit right at the top, fed live from Open-Meteo and calibrated against an 8-week validation harness. Tabs along the bottom cover Beaches, Trails, Family, Plan, and Town, so one link answers most of what a guest would otherwise walk to the desk to ask.

  • Per-beach safety flags, not one island-wide rating
  • Per-shore swell chips: North 1.3ft and East 5.8ft are different days
  • 7-day outlook so guests can plan the whole stay
  • Works on any phone as a web app; nothing to install
  • Favorites, so a guest's beach is one tap away all week

Chapter two

The Small Things That Earn a Guest's Trust

The details are why guests believe the app, and why your property looks careful for handing it to them.

Most weather widgets tell a guest there is a 60% chance of rain somewhere on the island, which is useless here. We tell them when: a “Rain til 6p” chip and a 12-hour precipitation strip. We pull live brown-water advisories from USGS stream gauges after heavy rain, and we run a box-jellyfish advisory off the lunar calendar. These are the calls a good lifeguard would make, put on a screen.

  • Rain timing (“Rain til 6p”), never a misleading island-wide percentage
  • Brown-water advisories fed by real USGS stream gauges
  • Box-jellyfish warnings timed to the lunar calendar
  • Hawaiian place names spelled correctly, diacriticals and all
  • Surf and wind feed stress-tested against 8 weeks of observed conditions

Chapter four

The Operator Dashboard: What You See

You do not have to take our word on usage — you watch it yourself.

The operator console shows your property's guest usage from real telemetry: a live Cloudflare collector, rolled up per property over 7, 30, and 90 days, including usage per occupied unit. Until your property connects, it runs in a clearly labeled PREVIEW mode; we would rather show you a labeled sample than a fake number. Your data is yours; there is no cross-hotel comparison.

  • Guest usage per occupied unit, over 7, 30, and 90 days
  • Guest taps are measured by a live collector, not estimated
  • Sample figures are stamped SAMPLE and previews say PREVIEW, so nothing reads as measured until it is
  • “No cross-hotel comparison” stated right on the console

Chapter five

The Town Tab: Guests Spend Nearby, Not Off the Map

Your concierge gets a vetted local guide, and local partners get a storefront guests can buy from in the app.

The Town tab is a curated local guide built on dozens of verified Kauai businesses, researched one by one rather than scraped. Shops can open a storefront right inside the app, with a dashboard showing sales and orders. Payments run through Square, and the money lands in the shop's own Square account, not ours.

  • Verified local businesses only; every listing checked by a person
  • In-app storefronts with product catalogs and order history
  • Square handles payment; funds go straight to the shop's own account
  • A better answer to “where should we eat?” than a laminated binder
