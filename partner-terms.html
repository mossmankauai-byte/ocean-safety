Partner Terms

Effective 11 August 2026 · Version 1.0

These terms apply to any business displaying, embedding, or distributing Ocean Safe — hotels, shops, vacation rentals and property managers, timeshares, town-ad listings, rental-car and Turo fleets, and concierges. They are what you agree to when you tick the box and sign your name on the signup form.

If you also have a separate signed agreement with Tropical Agronomics LLC dba Ocean Safe, that agreement wins wherever the two disagree. This page exists so your counsel can read it before you sign, not after.

1. What you get

A limited, revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to display and distribute your co-branded copy of Ocean Safe to your guests and customers — in-room cards, QR codes, your website, your app, your front desk, your vehicles.

You may use the Ocean Safe name and logo to identify the app as ours. You may not sublicense it, resell it, or hand it to another business.

What you pay, and what your tier includes, is whatever was shown to you at signup or in your signed agreement. Nothing on this page sets a price.

2. What you may not do

  • Present the app as your own product, or remove our attribution.
  • Alter the beach data, the safety ratings, the conditions logic, or the safety notice — any of it, ever.
  • Scrape, copy, or redistribute the dataset outside your licensed copy.
  • Suggest that Ocean Safe endorses your business beyond the co-branding itself.

3. The safety data is ours, and so is the responsibility for it

You are putting our beach ratings in front of your guests, under your logo. Your counsel is going to ask who is liable if a rating is wrong. Here is the answer, in writing, before they ask.

The ratings are ours. You didn't make them, you can't change them, and you're not certifying them. You are handing a guest a general guide.

The app carries its own disclaimer on every copy, including yours: it is a general guide, not a safety guarantee; it does not replace lifeguards, flags, signs, or official advisories; conditions change in minutes; the user assumes the risk. That notice is not removable, and that is deliberate — it is what keeps you a distributor rather than a certifier.

We do not warrant the data. Conditions information comes from third-party public services — NOAA, the National Weather Service, Open-Meteo, USGS — and may be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. The Service is provided "as is" and "as available," without warranties of any kind, express or implied, including merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, accuracy, or non-infringement. We don't warrant those third parties' data either.

Don't oversell it. Don't tell a guest a beach is safe because the app is green. Train your front desk the way you'd train them on any other advice: the app helps them plan, the lifeguard tells them about today. If your staff represents the app as a safety certification, that is your representation and not ours.

We indemnify you against claims that the app itself infringes someone's intellectual property. We do not indemnify you against your own staff telling a guest the water is fine.

To the fullest extent permitted by Hawaiʻi law, Ocean Safe is not liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or special damages arising from your use of the Service. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited under Hawaiʻi law.

4. Data — who decides what, and what you get

This section is the one to read closely.

We are the sole controller of guest-app data. We decide what is collected, how, for how long, and by which vendors. You do not configure it, you cannot turn parts of it on or off, and you obtain no rights in it. This is deliberate: it keeps your legal exposure at zero and keeps the guest's relationship with the app between the guest and us.

Our Privacy Policy governs your copy. Not yours. Every co-branded deployment links to it. Do not represent your own privacy policy as governing the app.

What we collect on your copy: a random visit id, whether the device is a phone or a computer, browser language, local hour, which tabs and places were opened, and — if your QR links carry a unit id — the room or unit number on the card. The full picture is in the Privacy Policy, and we'd rather you read it than take our summary.

What you get: aggregate counts only. Sessions, tabs opened, popular beaches, time-of-day patterns, trends. Never individual guest records. Never a guest-level export. Never a re-identifiable stream.

What you agree not to do with it:

  • Do not attempt to re-identify any visitor.
  • Do not join anything from Ocean Safe to your reservation system, PMS, CRM, loyalty database, or any other guest record. Room number plus timestamp plus your registration list identifies a family. Don't do it.
  • Do not use anything derived from the app to market to an identified guest.
  • Do not sell, license, or hand any of it to a third party.

These four bullets are the whole ballgame. The moment you can see guest-level behavior about your guests for your purposes, you become a joint controller of that data — jointly and severally liable, which means a regulator or a guest can come after you in full regardless of who caused the problem. We're not offering that. You don't want it.

5. Local listings may compete with you

Saying this here so nobody discovers it after signing.

The app recommends nearby shops, tours, and restaurants based on where the guest is and what the ocean is doing. Some of those may compete with your own rental desk, activity desk, or restaurant. Some pay us a commission.

We are not going to hide a good snorkel shop from a guest because you also rent fins. The recommendations serve the guest — that is the only reason a guest trusts the app, and the trust is the entire product. If they stop being for the guest, your copy is worth nothing to you either.

If that's a dealbreaker, say so now and we'll talk. Finding it yourself in month three is worse for both of us.

6. Your brand

You grant us a limited license to use your name and logo in your co-branded copy and, with your written OK, on a partner list. Nothing else. We won't put you in a case study without asking.

7. Features change

The app is under active development and its features will change. We may add, alter, or retire parts of it. The safety guide itself — the beach data, the ratings, and the conditions view — is carved out: we will not remove that from your copy while your agreement is in force.

If you are on a free tier, we'll give you reasonable notice of a material change; there is nothing to refund. If you pay, a material reduction gives you 30 days' notice and a penalty-free exit.

8. Ending it

Either of us can terminate on 30 days' written notice. We can terminate immediately for breach of §2, §3, or §4. If you pay monthly, canceling stops the next charge; we don't pro-rate a month already started.

On termination: stop distributing your copy, take down the QR codes and links, and stop using the Ocean Safe marks. We take your deployment offline and delete your aggregate analytics within 90 days, or sooner if you ask. Any aggregate counts already in your possession are yours to keep — there are no guest records in them, which is the point of §4.
