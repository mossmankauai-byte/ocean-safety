How we protect your guests

Restraint is the feature.

  • No names, unit numbers, IP, or User-Agent stored
  • No persistent user ID, no ad pixels, no session replay
  • Opaque 8-byte session IDs · Do-Not-Track respected
  • Your property’s data only — no leaderboards, no cross-property attribution
Everything here is your guests as a group — counts and percentages; we never store or report an individual guest, a device, or a unit. Think occupancy report, not surveillance. Counts are a floor by design: privacy-respecting (DNT) guests aren’t tracked, and first-opens undercount repeat in-stay use.

Places on your guest app

Curate the beaches, trails, food & shops your QR-branded app recommends.

Set each place to Featured (your house picks, surfaced first), Listed (in the app as normal), or hide it from your branded build. To start safe, hazardous spots come pre-set to ⊘ Not recommended — list them only if you mean to. Your choices export as a config file applied to your co-branded app only — they never change anyone else's. Nothing here is live guest data.

Safety guardrail. Hazardous spots — beaches with no lifeguard, warning-only beaches, and rip/surge channels — can be moved off your recommended lists (⊘ Not recommended), but can't be deleted from a safety app. Their hazard warning always stays visible if a guest searches for them. Calm, guarded beaches and all food/shops/trails can be hidden fully.

Repeat guest RES-RPT Repeat and direct-booking guests get the same guide and the same tour funnel, tuned to win the next stay direct.
Owner RES-OWN Owners get updates and a referral ask — no sales pitch, ever.

Reading the data views: every tile is tagged Measured / Modeled / Assumption / Sample. In this preview, all figures are Sample — illustrative placeholders, not your guests. Live mode renders only measured telemetry; a period with no data shows an empty state, never a seed number and never a movement figure against an empty prior week.

What makes it real: live telemetry from your property’s co-branded guest app — the pilot switches it on, and 60+ days of measured rollups replace every Sample figure. Until then the data views are a layout preview by design.
