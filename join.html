Common questions

The details, up front.

How fast can I be live?

Right away. You fill in the short form on this page and your co-branded page publishes as you finish — you get the link and your dashboard on the spot. Print-ready QR cards download from your dashboard.

What does it cost?

Starting is free — the co-branded page, live conditions and your dashboard cost nothing, and there is no card at signup. Paid plans depend on your business type — email us when you want one. Cancel anytime.

I don't sell products. Is this still worth it?

Yes. For hotels, inns, rentals, and timeshare operators the value is the safety touch itself — your name on the app every guest checks, branded cards in rooms and at the desk, and fewer worried calls. The store is optional.

Is the safety information reliable?

Conditions come from public NOAA and NWS sources and refresh daily. OceanSafe is guidance, not a guarantee — it doesn't replace lifeguards or posted advisories, and your page says so clearly.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Cancel anytime from your dashboard. Nothing renews unless you set up a paid plan yourself.
