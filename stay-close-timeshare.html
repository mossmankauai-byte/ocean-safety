Guardrails baked in

Owners never see a “buy ownership” pitch. Segment gating is enforced in the engine, not just the UI. Owners get upgrade + referral instead. Everything is opt-in, one ask + easy dismiss, and transparent that sessions are presentations. Disqualified non-owners get a friendly activity off-ramp, never a tour they can’t use. OceanSafe routes the qualified guest into the resort’s pipeline; it does not fulfill activities. Segment comes from the QR scanned; capacity (spots left) is set by the operator.
