Quick answers

Is it really free for my customers?

Yes — always. The app and your page cost visitors nothing. That's exactly why they use it before every beach day.

How do orders reach me?

Once your store is live, every paid order emails you the second it's placed — item, size, and a pickup code — and the money lands in your account. See "How orders will work" above.

What's the 10%?

10% of your in-app sales. For your first year we also cover the card-processing fees, so that 10% is truly all-in — nothing else comes out of a sale. Your page, conditions, and cards aren't taxed at all.

Do I pay anything to start?

No. Your first month is a free 1-month pilot, nothing to pay to start. You only get billed if you decide to continue after the month, and we'll check in with you first.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, just reply to any email. During your free pilot month there's nothing to cancel and nothing owed. No phone tree, no lock-in.
