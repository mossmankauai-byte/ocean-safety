OceanSafe

Be the local partner behind a funded beach-safety project on Kauaʻi

The County’s Kauaʻi Innovation Grant funds up to $50,000, but a for-profit can’t apply alone. It needs a Kauaʻi nonprofit as fiscal sponsor. That’s where you come in.

Innovation Grant · up to $50,000 · applications due Aug 6, 2026

OceanSafe is a free beach-safety guide for Kauaʻi. Live ocean conditions, hazard warnings, and family-friendly picks, in one app people actually open at the beach. We’re applying for the County’s Innovation Grant to expand it. To apply, we need a Kauaʻi nonprofit to hold the grant. In return, your group gets money, reach, and standing, and we do the technical work.

What you get

Three things, all real, none of them busywork.

1

Unrestricted funding

$2,500–$5,000

Fiscal sponsors keep a standard admin fee, usually 5–10% of the award. On a $50,000 grant that’s $2,500 to $5,000 your group can spend on anything, no strings.

2

Reach at the beach

Your co-branded version of the app puts your cleanups, volunteer sign-ups, and donate button in front of people the moment they pick a beach. That’s your audience, exactly when they care.

3

Standing with the County

You become the named sponsor of a funded County project. That’s a real relationship with the Office of Economic Development, and a line you can point to for years.

What you carry, and how we make it easy

Being the fiscal sponsor comes with two responsibilities. We built the tool that handles the hard one.

You’re the legal grantee

You sign the grant agreement and the money flows through your books to the project, under a simple fiscal-sponsorship agreement. Standard nonprofit work.

You report to the County

Grants come with reporting. Our partner dashboard writes it for you: sessions, safety-warning views, cleanups held, volunteer sign-ups. Export it as a CSV or a print-clean page and hand it in.

The dashboard does this for you

How it works

Two pieces, both ready to look at today.

The co-branded guide

The same beach-safety app, in your name. “In partnership with your group,” your cleanups in the plan, a steward badge on the beaches you care for, and volunteer and donate buttons.

  • Safety data never changes. Warnings and hazards stay exactly as they are.
  • Your events show up where people are already planning their day.

The partner dashboard

Add cleanups, pick the beaches you steward, set your branding, and pull your County report. Everything a grant reviewer wants to see, in one place.

  • See how many people used your guide and read a safety warning.
  • One click for the reporting summary.
