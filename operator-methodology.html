How the data flows

One in-room scan to one number on this page. No name is ever collected; nothing on the path can be tied back to a guest.

The event carries a metric name and a day bucket — never a name, email, room number, or a retained device identifier. The Worker keeps one rolled-up row per slug per day in Cloudflare KV; the console reads those rows and merges them in your browser. Nothing on this path can re-identify a guest, and no figure is computed until its minimum-sample floor is cleared.

What we do not claim

  • No bookings, no revenue. Every count is a tap of interest. We never report a booking, a reservation, or captured revenue — the dollar figure is a modeled floor on interest, not money earned.
  • No per-guest tracking. There is no guest profile, no cross-session identity, and no room-level activity — QR codes identify a property, never a room or a person.
  • Interest, not outcome. A render is a panel shown; a click, tap, or lead is interest expressed — never proof a guest went somewhere, spent anything, or that a hazard was prevented.
  • No benchmark dressed up as your result. Modeled and Assumption inputs are tagged as such on every figure that uses them; they are never presented as your property's measured data.
