Why this works better for you than for a hotel

  • Your renter gets no orientation. A keyless or remote handoff means nobody tells them which beaches have rip currents this week. A hotel has a front desk for that; you don't. The QR is the cheapest duty-of-care answer you can hand someone.
  • You are the first vendor they meet — often booked weeks out, long before any concierge desk.
  • The car is the trunk. Gear sells to a driver because they can actually carry it.
  • On Maui and Kauaʻi, airport delivery restrictions push you to off-site meetups — a few minutes of standing around that is pure attention, and currently earns you nothing.
  • Every renter drives off asking "where do we go first" — you can be the one who answers it.

Which one you actually want

On an engaged fleet the free tier often pays us more than $5/car does, so we are not pushing you off it. Go paid when you want the store in front of your renters.

How the money actually moves

You are the merchant of record. The sale settles into your own Square account, in your name, on your books. We never run the sale on your register and never hold your money.

Straight answers to the three questions you'll ask

  • "Do I have to change my POS?" No. Day one runs off a spreadsheet of your gear and a printed or emailed pickup slip — that works with any register you own. Live sync with Square, Shopify, Clover, or Lightspeed is available on request; those connectors are built per partner, not self-serve today.
  • "Who handles deposits?" You do, inside your own Square, exactly as you do now. We deliberately keep security deposits off our rail so nothing of yours is ever tied up in our system.
  • "Who texts my customer?" You do, from your own account. We never text your guests and never store their phone numbers.
