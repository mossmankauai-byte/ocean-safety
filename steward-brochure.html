OceanSafe
Free · yours to use

A beach guide with your name on it, and a simple way to run it

OceanSafe is a free beach-safety app for Kauaʻi, Oʻahu, Maui, and the Big Island. This is your own co-branded version, plus a dashboard to manage it. No cost, nothing to sign.

People already open OceanSafe when they head to the beach, for live conditions and hazard warnings. Your version puts your cleanups, the beaches you look after, and your volunteer and donate links right there too. You run it from a simple dashboard, and you can pull an impact report whenever you need one.

Two pieces

One for beachgoers, one for you.

Your guide

The OceanSafe app, in your name. What beachgoers see:

  • Your cleanups show up in the app’s plan and around town, with an RSVP.
  • Beaches you care for get a small “cared for by you” badge.
  • Volunteer and donate buttons, going to your own links.
  • All of it sits on top of the live safety info, which never changes.

Your dashboard

A plain admin page. What you do there:

  • Add and edit cleanup events in a few seconds.
  • Pick the beaches you steward and set your branding.
  • See how many people used your guide and read a safety warning.
  • Pull a one-click impact report as a spreadsheet or a clean printout.

Why it’s free

More people getting real safety information, and more hands on the beach, is the whole point. Your name on the guide helps both. That’s the deal, there isn’t a catch.

The safety layer is never yours to change, and never ours to sell. Hazard data, warnings, and the disclaimer stay exactly as they are, on every version of the app.

Start in three steps

  1. Open your dashboard and add your first cleanup.
  2. Pick the beaches you look after and drop in your links.
  3. Print your QR code and put it where beachgoers see it.
